Abstract:
Chromosome segmentation is essential for karyotyping analysis, which provides a 
reliable basis for subsequent disease diagnosis. Recent deep segmentation techniques 
can significantly boost the efficiency of chromosome micrographs processing. 
However, existing classical Mask R-CNN-based methods cannot deal with 
chromosome overlapping issues highly susceptible to appearing in actual data. In this 
paper, we argue that it is difficult for models to achieve accurate segmentation without 
a specific design for complex overlapping. Therefore, we propose a divide-and-conquer 
(DaC) automatic framework, which has better robustness for complex chromosome 
instance segmentation. An overlapping judgment branch is designed to determine 
whether the involved chromosomes overlap. To avoid missing detection, multi-instance 
prediction (MIP) is introduced for overlapping ones. Next, an overlap-non-overlap 
attention segmentation module, which facilitates non-local awareness of different parts 
of chromosomes and is cascaded with a mask head, is proposed to decouple the 
relationship between overlapping chromosomes. Finally, we design a shape prior 
knowledge module for the pipeline to obtain reliable mask predictions. We also 
construct a novel overlapping chromosome annotation (OCA) module to train the above 
model effectively to obtain high-quality overlapping annotations. The experimental 
results show that the proposed method achieves mean Average Precision (mAP) of

1. Introduction
Karyotype is the phenotype of chromosomes at mid-mitosis. There are 22 pairs of 
autosomes and a pair of sex chromosomes in each healthy human cell[1]. Any 
numerical abnormality or structural aberration can lead to genetic disorders such as 
mental retardation [2] and congenital malformations[3]. Karyotype analysis identifies 
chromosomal abnormalities and is a tool for diagnosing genetic diseases and prenatal 
screening of pregnant women[4]. Traditional karyotype analysis is purely manual, 
making the process tedious and complex. Automation and intelligence are necessary to 
improve the efficiency of karyotype analysis [5]. Chromosome segmentation is the first 
step in automated karyotype analysis and is the foundation for subsequent chromosome 
classification and analysis. The primary objective is to separate the background from 
the foreground in mid-term cell microscopy images, thereby extracting individual 
chromosomes [6]. However, chromosomes are non-rigid, so multiple chromosomes 
will overlap or adhere to each other, which requires a time-consuming, costly, and 
manual effort to complete chromosome segmentation. Therefore, developing an 
effective solution for segmenting overlapping chromosomes is urgent.
Early studies proposed using geometric morphology-based methods to segment 
overlapping chromosomes [7]. These methods extracted features such as concave and 
convex points and skeletons and achieved some success in automated chromosome 
segmentation. However, they relied on specific segmentation rules that anomalies and 
noise could disrupt. Additionally, the process was complex and time-consuming, and 
the accuracy and generalization ability needed improvement.
More recently, deep learning has led to the emergence of automatic chromosome 
segmentation models based on convolutional neural networks. State-of-the-art 
approaches for chromosome instance segmentation typically use the Mask R-CNN [8] 
paradigm, which has more potent generalization abilities than traditional algorithms 
and can automatically learn object features to complete segmentation tasks. However,

chromosomes overlap. For overlapping ones, we introduce multi-instance prediction 
(MIP) of overlapping chromosomes in the object detection stage to obtain detection 
boxes for all overlapping chromosomes, and the overlap-induced missed detection 
problem is avoided by optimizing NMS. To provide a basis for subsequent decoupling

above algorithm, we can explicitly model the overlapping and non-overlapping areas

noise or overlapping chromosome mask conflicts effectively, we propose a prior 
knowledge module to guide the segmentation of the model. Our carefully designed 
DaCSeg achieves higher accuracy for end-to-end overlapping chromosome instance 
segmentation. The main contributions of this paper are summarized as follows:  
(1) To obtain a well-built overlapping chromosome dataset, we propose a universal 
algorithm that automatically marks the overlapping region of chromosomes, called 
overlapping chromosome annotation (OCA), which can be extended to other instance 
segmentation datasets.
(2) To divide and conquer the overlapping chromosomes, we build an overlapping 
judgment branch to detect whether chromosome overlap occurs and present an 
improved MIP for overlapping ones. Next, we propose an overlap-non-overlap 
attention segmentation module to explore non-local connections using self-attention 
mechanisms to unravel and explore the overlapping relationship between chromosomes 
and better capture the information of highly overlapping regions for mask prediction.
(3) To further correct the segmentation predictions and drive the network towards 
rationalization, we propose a shape prior guided segmentation module, which captures 
chromosome generic template shapes based on the behavior of human perception to 
train the model behavior.

Most instance segmentation approaches typically adopt Mask R-CNN as their baseline. 
Mask R-CNN adds a segmentation branch on top of Faster R-CNN[16] and, therefore,

chromosome segmentation problem. The results showed that Mask R-CNN was the

direct application of the baseline model made some overlapping chromosomes still not

of the baseline model in overlapping cases. Mask Scoring R-CNN [21] introduces a

finer instance segmentation in the overlapping nuclei. 
3. Methods
Our approach is motivated by the observation that chromosome segmentation errors 
tend to occur at the locations where chromosome clusters overlap rather than in regions 
where single chromosomes are present and do not overlap. However, existing classical 
Mask R-CNN-based methods fail to address overlapping chromosome challenges 
effectively. We argue that divide-and-conquer for overlapping chromosomes and 
breaking the challenges down step-by-step can carefully improve segmentation 
accuracy. The framework of the new model is presented in Fig. 2. Firstly, we propose 
the overlapping chromosome annotation (OCA) algorithm to get the regions of 
overlapping and non-overlapping masks. As for the model pipeline, we design an 
overlapping judgment branch to determine whether chromosomes overlap. It helps us 
incorporate multi-instance prediction (MIP) on the overlapping chromosomes during 
the object detection stage, reducing the likelihood of missed detections for crossing-
overlap chromosomes and providing better detection results. With the overlapping 
annotations provided by the OCA algorithm, we design the overlap-non-overlap 
attention segmentation module to mine the overlapping region more accurately, which

3.2 Multi-Instance Prediction of Overlapping Chromosomes

when chromosomes overlap. Three proposal boxes(green, blue, and red) are heavily

empty), which is intrinsically difficult as the proposals share very similar features. 
Moreover, only one prediction likely survives after NMS. Therefore, following the 
strategies used in[28], we design multiple instance predictions in the overlapped 
chromosome detection. Not one proposal predicts one chromosome, but the 
overlapping chromosomes are also predicted.

3.4 The Shape Prior Guiding Segmentation Module

segment chromosome masks using shape-prior knowledge of chromosomes. We 
observed that although chromosomes are usually shown as bent or deformed in the 
metaphase image, they can be summarized as regular schemes[34], usually displayed 
as thin strips in the border box of the marker, and positioned along the diagonal or 
horizontal or vertical direction, as shown in Fig.7(a). Therefore, it is reasonable to 
introduce several general-template masks to represent patterns of chromosomes. 
  In the pre-training phase, to model the shape prior, we collect the ground-truth masks 
in the training set and use an auto-encoder to obtain the embedding of each mask. Then, 
we partition the embeddings into 5 clusters via K-Means clustering and use the centers 
of these clusters as a codebook. Thus, the codebook could memorize the embeddings

4.4.2 Results Analysis 
From the results in Table 2, we can observe that DaCSeg can perform better in terms 
of mean average precision and other metrics, demonstrating its effectiveness for 
overlapping chromosome segmentation. The performance of the basic framework on 
mAP is 79.42%, AP50 is 97.42%, and AP75 is 93.61%. Since PANet follows the 
hyperparameters of Mask R-CNN, the segmentation results of PANet are not as good 
as Mask R-CNN. The other general instance segmentation methods, Cascade R-CNN, 
HTC, and Mask scoring R-CNN, compared to Mask R-CNN, have a certain degree of 
improvement. BCNet pioneered the application of the Non-local module and Bilayer 
Occlusion module in the mask head, which performs better than general instance 
segmentation methods in overlapping chromosome segmentation scenarios. AS-PANet

datasets, and both achieve better results than Mask R-CNN. The mAP has improved by 
0.72% and 3.68%, AP50 has improved by 0.21% and 0.3%, and AP75 has improved

compared with Mask R-CNN. Besides, AP50 and AP75 have a notable improvement 
compared to other related methods, mainly due to our reasonable network design rather 
than the network's direct expansion.

5. Conclusion
This paper proposes a novel end-to-end instance segmentation framework, DaCSeg, 
to tackle the issue of complex overlapping chromosome segmentation. Firstly, we 
present the OCA algorithm for obtaining overlapping chromosome ground truth masks 
and design an overlapping judgment branch to determine if chromosomes overlap in 
the model framework. Subsequently, we conduct further network design for 
overlapping chromosomes. We incorporate the MIP and Set NMS strategy to prevent 
detection boxes of overlapping chromosomes from being filtered out as redundant. 
With such detection results, to mitigate severe overlapping issues of chromosome 
instance masks, we innovatively propose an overlap-non-overlap attention module 
inspired by the attention mechanism to decouple overlapping relationships and provide 
abundant overlapping auxiliary predictive information for the final segmentation of 
overlapping chromosomes. Additionally, we design a shape prior guiding segmentation 
module, inspired by the perceptual behavior of karyotype experts, to enhance the mask 
This preprint research paper has not been peer reviewed. Electronic copy available at: https://ssrn.com/abstract=4570895
Preprint not peer reviewed

predictions and steer the segmentation network towards reasonable shape segmentation.
Experimental results show that DaCSeg can capture the relationship between 
overlapping chromosomes, suppress ambiguity caused by similar features of 
overlapping chromosomes, and achieve more accurate segmentation results in cases of 
chromosome overlap. However, accuracy and internal consumption must also be 
considered in practical clinical applications. In the future, the model requires pruning 
to enhance computational speed and efficiency while maintaining improved accuracy.
